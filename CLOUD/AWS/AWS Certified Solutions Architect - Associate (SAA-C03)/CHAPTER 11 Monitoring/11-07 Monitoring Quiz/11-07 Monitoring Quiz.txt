QUESTION 5

A period is the length of time associated with a specific Amazon CloudWatch statistic. What is the default period value?


600 seconds


300 seconds


10 seconds

CORRECT
60 seconds

Sorry!
The default Amazon CloudWatch period value is 60 seconds.

Correct Answer
A period is the length of time associated with a specific Amazon CloudWatch statistic. Each statistic represents an aggregation of the metrics data collected for a specified period of time. Periods are defined in numbers of seconds, and valid values for period are 1, 5, 10, 30, or any multiple of 60. For example, to specify a period of six minutes, use 360 as the period value. You can adjust how the data is aggregated by varying the length of the period. A period can be as short as one second or as long as one day (86,400 seconds). The default value is 60 seconds. Reference: Periods (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudWatch/latest/monitoring/cloudwatch_concepts.html#CloudWatchPeriods).
