What is a PaaS or a Platform as a Service?
This is an application deployment model
that simplifies the process for developers
to deploy and manage their applications.
With a PaaS, you supply your code,
and the provider takes care of building,
deploying, and managing the underlying infrastructure.
It really allows you to focus on
the app development and functionality.
Essentially, it's a one-stop solution
for streamlined application deployment and management.
Now in the AWS ecosystem,
Elastic Beanstalk is considered the go-to platform
for your Platform as a Service need.
With Elastic Beanstalk,
developers are able to focus on app development
all while AWS handles the underlying infrastructure.

Node.js, Ruby, and Go.
