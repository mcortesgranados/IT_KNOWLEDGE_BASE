Well, what is Amazon DocumentDB?
Well, basically, it allows you
to run MongoDB on the AWS cloud.

that scales with your workloads
and safely and durably stores your database information.
So basically, Amazon DocumentDB is just a way
of running MongoDB workloads on the AWS cloud.
